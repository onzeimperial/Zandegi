- (insight) Baseline: mission = chapters of steps; step confirmed by check (40 XP) or photo (50 XP); chapter bonus 2.5x its steps, mission bonus 4x largest chapter; XP folded from append-only ledger
- (direction by user) Problem: progression needs to be more rewarding and better structured
- (direction by user) Techniques chosen: Role Playing, First Principles Thinking, Assumption Reversal, Laddering, How Might We
- (technique) started Role Playing
- (idea by user) Checkpoint test every few weeks: a small real test of the expected position (e.g. 3 weeks from 60kg bench -> attempt 65kg), worth extra XP and acting as a motivation point
- (idea by coach) Checkpoint test as a chapter 'boss fight': the chapter only closes when you pass a real-world trial. Metric goals test a number (65kg); outcome goals test a real act (order a meal in Farsi, sit a timed practice exam)
- (idea by coach) The checkpoint target is forecast up front by a published formula from your starting point, so you see the 65kg coming from week 1 and it pulls you forward
- (idea by user) Failing a boss fight is still progress: any attempt above your baseline is something to be proud of, however small the increase
- (idea by user) After a boss fight, a short debrief: tips, what went well, what didn't, what you'll improve for the next boss fight
- (idea by coach) Boss fight XP pays on gain over baseline (a PR), not pass/fail; a miss schedules a rematch instead of locking the chapter
- (idea by coach) The debrief becomes a battle log the next chapter actually reads: 'what I'll improve' turns into the first steps of the next chapter, so reflection visibly reshapes the plan
- (idea by user) Group boss fights: everyone in the crew must complete a small checkpoint goal to defeat a shared boss, making progress communal
- (idea by coach) Crew boss has an HP bar; each member's checkpoint on their OWN goal deals damage (bench PR, 5km time, Farsi conversation) - different goals, one shared enemy
- (idea by coach) Damage scales with each person's gain relative to their own baseline, so a beginner's +2.5kg hits as hard as an expert's, keeping it fair across goals and levels
- (idea by user) A failed crew raid can be retried after 2 days, so no one is the permanent reason the crew lost
- (idea by coach) During the 2-day gap crewmates can send 'assists' (a tip from their debrief, joining a session) that count toward the rematch, turning pressure into help
- (idea by user) Cheat: photograph a bench loaded with 90kg without lifting it - a static photo proves the setup, not the effort
- (idea by coach) Plausibility check: a published formula flags a boss result far outside your trajectory (62.5kg -> 90kg in 2 weeks) and asks for stronger proof instead of paying out
- (insight by coach) Verification strength should scale with stakes: a tick is fine for a daily step, boss fights and leaderboard-affecting results need stronger proof
- (idea by user) Boss fight proof by video, matched to the goal: bench = video of the lift, Farsi = video of them speaking, business = video/evidence of clients closed
- (idea by coach) Challenge code: the app shows a random word/number to say or show at the start of the video, so an old or borrowed clip can't be reused
- (idea by coach) Integrations as proof for hard-to-film goals: Stripe/Basiq revenue for business, Strava for running, GitHub for coding - the real world's own records verify it
- (idea by coach) Witness proof: a crewmate or gym buddy vouches for the lift, staking a little of their own credibility - no camera needed
- (idea by coach) Device data as proof for private goals: phone Screen Time for doom-scrolling, sleep data for sleep goals
- (idea by coach) Self-attested boss fights are allowed but are 'private-tier': full personal XP and story, but don't count toward leaderboards or crew raids
- (technique) started First Principles Thinking
- (insight by coach) Bedrock: people quit long goals in the middle (weeks 4-10), not at the start or near the finish
- (insight by user) Bedrock: seeing friends progress makes people more likely to progress themselves
- (insight by user) Bedrock: if the goal feels too hard people stop - AI-generated checkpoints can be set too hard
- (idea by coach) Checkpoint targets are never set by the AI (law 5): a published formula sets them from your baseline and actual rate, and recalibrates down after misses so the next boss is always winnable-but-hard
- (idea by coach) Crew progress feed of PRs and boss kills, not XP totals - you see friends actually getting better
- (idea by user) A badge around week 6 (the middle of the goal) to keep people going
- (idea by coach) Midpoint badge that carries evidence: it shows your week-1 vs week-6 numbers or clips side by side, so the badge itself is proof of change, displayable on your profile
- (idea by user) Badges displayed on your profile and must feel very prestigious
- (idea by coach) Badge prestige tied to proof: the gold version of a badge only exists for video/witness/integration-verified results; self-reported earns the plain version
- (idea by coach) Real rarity stat on each badge: 'held by 6% of people who started this goal' - computed from real data, never invented
- (idea by coach) Limited profile showcase slots (e.g. 3) so you curate your proudest badges, which makes each displayed badge mean more
- (idea by user) Reversal: user logs what they did instead of following app-given steps - captures smaller increments of progress, boosting motivation
- (idea by user) Allow every little piece of progress to be uploaded to the app
- (idea by coach) Quick-capture 'log anything' button: a one-line note/photo/number; the app files it under the right chapter automatically, so unplanned wins (an extra session, a Farsi chat with a cab driver) still count
- (idea by coach) Small logs build an evidence trail (the story of your goal) rather than paying big XP, so spamming tiny uploads can't farm the leaderboard
- (idea by user) Much of the planning should be user-modifiable, because the user knows their own situation best
- (idea by coach) AI drafts, user owns: beginners take the draft plan as-is, experienced users rewrite, reorder, add or delete steps freely
- (insight by coach) If the big XP comes from boss fights (verified, formula-set), editing steps can't be used to farm XP - so users can be given full freedom over their plan safely
- (idea by user) Reversal: the system assumes users always have free time - but school commitments and exam periods mean less time for the goal; progression should account for busy periods
- (idea by coach) Maintenance mode: declare a busy period (exams) and the goal drops to a tiny 'hold the line' version - streak and progress protected, boss fights rescheduled after
- (idea by coach) Calendar-aware pacing: Google Calendar integration spots exam weeks / heavy weeks ahead and reshapes the chapter schedule before you fall behind
- (idea by user) Hold-the-line during busy periods: something tiny like watching a video about the goal still counts
- (idea by coach) Maintenance menu of 5-minute 'touch' actions per goal: a bench technique video, a Farsi podcast on the bus, 10 flashcards - keeps the thread alive, protects the streak, pays small XP
- (technique) started Laddering
- (insight by user) Ladder rung 1: a rewarding system gives motivation to complete the next goal and to stay on the app
- (insight by user) Ladder rung 2: completing the goal gives self-satisfaction and reinforcement from her social group (social media, friends' group chat)
- (insight by user) Ladder top: the real need is self-efficacy - believing she can grow herself as a person. Rewarding progression = evidence that you can change
- (idea by coach) Past wins carry forward: when a new goal gets hard, Zandegi shows your own proof from a past goal ('you went 60kg -> 100kg in 14 weeks') - your history becomes the motivation
- (idea by coach) Shareable boss-fight cards (before/after, verified badge) built for the group chat and socials, since recognition actually happens outside the app
- (idea by user) Teach the labelling technique: the app prompts users to label themselves positively (e.g. on the bench goal, 'I'm someone who perseveres and doesn't give up') to build self-belief
- (idea by coach) Earned identity titles: labels unlock from real behaviour patterns, e.g. 'The Unbroken' after returning for 3 rematches, 'Early Riser' after 20 dawn sessions - the label is backed by your own evidence so it doesn't feel hollow
- (idea by coach) Title shown under your name on your profile and in crew raids, like an RPG class - your identity is the reward, not the XP number
- (question by coach) HMW make failing a boss fight feel like levelling up? / HMW make proof feel like a proud moment, not paperwork? / HMW keep people going through the boring middle weeks?
- (question by user) HMW make progress feel like a communal activity, with the group as a team?
- (idea by coach) Spotter pairs: each crew member is paired with one other who gets notified of their boss fight, can assist, and earns a share when they win - small, personal accountability inside the crew
- (idea by coach) Crew season map: every member's chapter clears move the whole crew along one shared journey map, with crew-only cosmetics unlocked at landmarks
- (idea by user) Group badges as crew levels: higher tiers named like 'Super Team', 'All-Star Team'
- (idea by coach) Crew tier needs everyone: rank rises on raids cleared where every member contributed, so one strong member can't carry the crew to All-Star
- (idea by coach) Crew-vs-crew leagues: crews of similar tier face off each season on boss damage (relative to baselines), giving teams a rival to rally against
- (idea by user) Full-crew week bonus: if everyone in the crew completes their missions that week, everyone gets an overall XP boost
- (idea by coach) Crew streak: consecutive full-crew weeks stack the boost up to a published cap; a busy-period member in maintenance mode still counts as 'complete' so exams don't break the crew
- (idea by coach) Sunday crew huddle ritual: a weekly recap screen showing every member's PRs, boss damage, and debrief highlights - the moment the boost is revealed together
- (direction by user) User ended ideation at wrap and asked how to integrate these changes and start full app development
- (decision by user) MVP scope: solo first. Boss fights, debriefs, rematches, editable plans, log anything, busy-period mode, earned titles go in; crews, video, integrations become first post-MVP release
- (insight by coach) Spec conflicts found at wrap-up: crews/leaderboards/integrations are non-goals; video outside evidence allowlist; full-crew XP bonus conflicts with 'engagement never increases XP'; touch actions cannot pay XP; editable plans can farm step XP under neutral-v1 unless capped
