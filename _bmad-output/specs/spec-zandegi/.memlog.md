- (direction) Reconcile the legacy product specification and build prompts to the finalized architecture; the architecture spine is authoritative on conflicts.
- (capability) CAP-1: A person can submit a free-text goal and receive a classified Pursuit match or safe generic scaffold; success is a persisted goal with explicit confidence and candidate capture below threshold.
- (capability) CAP-2: A person can receive a safe, sourced, useful Mission through validated progressive reveal; success is one accepted complete Mission with no raw or rejected model output exposed.
- (capability) CAP-3: Administrators can author and validate the 140-Pursuit seed catalog; success is full schema, safety, source, uniqueness, and coverage validation with no stubs.
- (capability) CAP-4: A person can complete eligible work and receive deterministic server-awarded progression; success is idempotent ledger, award, projection, outbox, replay, and compensation behaviour.
- (capability) CAP-5: A person can use registered tools and private evidence to support completion; success is a qualifying server-attested fact, not mere tool use, with ownership and lifecycle enforcement.
- (capability) CAP-6: A person can schedule and reschedule Mission steps using Zandegi's internal scheduler; success is persisted Blocks without any external calendar dependency.
- (capability) CAP-7: A guest can experience the core loop and claim progress into Clerk identity once; success is atomic, idempotent claim without lost or duplicated progress.
- (capability) CAP-8: A person can use the complete MVP loop on the web; success is a guest-to-account vertical slice through generation, completion, progression, evidence, scheduling, and deletion/export.
- (capability) CAP-9: Operators can review Pursuit candidates, catalog content, safety failures, dead letters, evidence states, and projection rebuilds; success is least-privilege auditable workflows.
- (capability) CAP-10: A person can export and delete personal data while ledger integrity remains pseudonymous; success is a retryable cross-provider deletion workflow with completion receipt.
- (constraint) Progression formulas and tuning live in core; model estimates, payments, subscriptions, purchasable currency, and entitlements never influence XP, streaks, rank, or competitive position.
- (constraint) MVP evidence kinds are SELF, TIMER, ARTIFACT, and METRIC; INTEGRATION awards nothing until a provider-specific post-MVP attestation policy is approved.
- (constraint) All third-party integrations including external calendar sync, plus mobile, billing, and social/competitive systems, are outside the MVP.
- (constraint) Private Vercel Blob is the MVP evidence store behind an API-owned storage port; regulated documents are out of scope.
- (constraint) Personal goal and Mission content remains deletable; it never enters the immutable progression/economy ledger.
- (constraint) The MVP uses the modular-monolith package boundaries, Unit of Work, versioned contracts, transactional outbox, Sydney deployment, and environment isolation defined by the architecture spine.
- (constraint) Safety, age restrictions, evidence trust, authorization, and validated streaming are enforced by the server, not only by clients or models.
- (decision) The canonical spec lives at _bmad-output/specs/spec-zandegi; docs/SPEC.md becomes a compatibility pointer so two canonical specifications cannot drift.
- (decision) The legacy 140-Pursuit list, goal-display rules, Mission shape, tools, safety rules, and design commitments are preserved in spec companions; incompatible formulas, monetization, integrations, and build ordering are superseded.
- (question) Before Phase 4 schema freeze, decide guest retention duration, renewal activity, warning timing, and evidence cleanup treatment.
- (question) Before progression implementation, product owners must approve exact neutral rewards, authored bands, caps, level curve, and provisional-review thresholds without using model estimates.
- (event) Express-mode update selected because the existing product spec, build prompts, and finalized architecture provide sufficient intent.
- (decision) Created product-rules.md, pursuit-catalog.md, and source-reconciliation.md so all retained, superseded, deferred, or unresolved legacy requirements have an explicit destination.
- (event) Coherence validation passed: 10 stable capabilities each have intent and success; constraints, non-goals, concrete success signal, two explicit open questions, and lean companion routing satisfy Spec Law.
- (event) Preservation validation passed: the old SPEC was walked by section; retained product rules and catalog were preserved, architecture conflicts were explicitly superseded, post-MVP material was explicitly deferred, and the former build sequence was replaced.
- (event) Spec finalized after reconciliation with the architecture and docs/BUILD-PROMPTS.md.
