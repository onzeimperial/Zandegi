## Catalog safety notes

Catalog authoring must classify clinical, financial, legal, age-restricted, and self-harm-adjacent risk explicitly. The original labels for `chronic-condition-management` and `anxiety-management` as clinical are preserved, but validation must assess every Pursuit rather than relying only on those two examples.
